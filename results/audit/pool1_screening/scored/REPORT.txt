               +1 perfect, 0 no better than a random shuffle, -1 fully reversed.
  pairs ok     (1 + tau) / 2: share of all fragment pairs put in the right
               relative order. 50% = random shuffle.
  exact        share of fragments sitting in exactly their true position.
  shift        average number of positions a fragment is away from where it
               belongs. 0 = perfect.
  longest run  the longest stretch that is consecutive in the original AND
               placed consecutively in the right order: the biggest piece of
               the document the attacker got back intact. "of N" = fragments
               per document.
  run%         longest run / fragments in the document: the share of the
               document recovered as one intact stretch.

GAME J -- nothing is given. The attacker gets every fragment of a pool of 7
records mixed together and must sort them into records AND order each one.
Scored on PAIRS of fragments.
  link P       linkage precision: of the pairs the model put in the same record,
               the share that really come from the same record.
  link R       linkage recall: of the pairs that really share a record, the
               share the model put together.
  link F1      harmonic mean of link P and link R: how well it separated the
               records, ignoring order.
  q            direction accuracy: of the CORRECTLY linked pairs, the share it
               put in the right order. 0.5 = coin flip.
  joint P      of every pair the model claimed, the share that are same-record
               AND in the right order.
  joint R      of every true same-record pair, the share the model recovered in
               the right order.
  joint F1     harmonic mean of joint P and joint R. THE HEADLINE. It equals
               link F1 x q, so the two columns show which half the defence won.
  chance       the joint F1 of a trivial attacker that puts every fragment in
               one pile in random order. Computed exactly for each pool.
  adv          advantage over chance = (joint F1 - chance) / (1 - chance).
               0 = chance, 1 = perfect reconstruction, below 0 = worse than chance.
  gate         integrity gate. "ok" only if every document/pool has a real model
               answer and no answer needed more than 25% of its fragments placed
               by the scorer. Otherwise the cell is REFUSED, never scored around.

TERMS IN THE PER-CELL REPORTS
  fragments placed by policy  share of fragments the model left out that the
               scorer placed itself (as singletons / appended). Over 25% refuses.
  malformed    game O answers that are not a usable ordering; excluded, counted.
  COVERAGE     share of fragments the model placed; "dropped" ones become
               singletons, which claim no pairs (so they cost recall, not precision).
  groups guessed vs asked   records the model formed vs the 7 in the pool.
  F1 BREAKDOWN pair counts behind link P/R, q and joint P/R: pairs guessed,
               pairs true, K = guessed pairs that really share a record,
               C = those K that also point the right way. q = C / K.
  q_micro      q from pairs summed over pools (same as the q column at 1 pool).
  tau in matched groups   DIAGNOSTIC, not a headline: each guessed record is
               matched to its true record and tau is taken on their overlap.
  records recovered EXACTLY   records the model got completely right: every
               fragment, nothing extra, perfect order.
  REFERENCE    game O's tau put on J's scale, (1 + tau) / 2, for a like-for-like
               read. Not a ceiling and not a decomposition.

SAMPLE SIZE: pool 1 only -- 7 documents per cell for O, 1 pool per cell for J.
An operational slice of the grid, not a result.
